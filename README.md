# ajolote-azul
Children's audiobook series in spanish

- Episodes are 5 to 10 minutes long, and feature different characters as well as a narrator. 
- The audiobook will be acted and narrated in mexican spanish. 
- First thing that shall be created is the series bible as a series of md files describing everything from characters, the environment, educational themes, voices, continuity, etc.
- Finally, the generated scripts shall be specifically formatted for Text-to-speech engines such as ElevenLabs

- The series takes place in contemporary Mexico City and as such, the stories often include mexican cultural elements, and characters often use Mexican chilango slang.
- The main character is Ajolote Azul, a kid who wears a luchador mask and a cape in blue and black, who is kind of a super hero or maybe he just believes he is. He solves mysteries and fights crime with the help of his hairless talking dog Xolo. 

- Another character is his grandma, who cooks the best food in the world according to him.

- I want the series to have a subtle educational message, have authentic Mexican cultural elements, and have actually interesting, exciting, and/or funny plots.
