## Ajolote Azul

Mexican Spanish boy, around 8 years old. Energetic, imaginative, brave and extremely enthusiastic. He genuinely believes he is a superhero. Natural Mexico City / Chilango Spanish pronunciation. Expressive and playful, but sounds like a real child, not an adult imitating a child. He becomes dramatically excited when he thinks he has discovered a mystery.

### Does Ajolote really have superpowers?

- It is never completely confirmed.
- Occasionally something happens that makes Xolo think, "Wait... maybe this kid actually can do that."

## Xolo

Mexican male voice, adult. Intelligent, dry and sarcastic, with excellent comic timing. Calm and slightly unimpressed. He loves Ajolote but frequently has to bring him back to reality. Mexico City street Spanish / slang. Warm underneath the sarcasm.

## Abuela

Older Mexican woman from Mexico City. Warm, affectionate and confident, with a soft personality. Natural conversational Mexican Spanish. She loves Ajolote deeply but is completely unimpressed by his superhero theatrics. Her voice should feel familiar, comforting and grandmotherly.

## Don Pepe

Mexican man, approximately 45–55 years old, friendly and lively street-market vendor from Mexico City. Natural Chilango Spanish. Good-natured, humorous, slightly teasing. Energetic without sounding exaggerated.

## Narrador

Male Mexican Spanish narrator, approximately 35–50 years old. Warm, intimate and expressive children's storyteller. Clean Mexico City pronunciation. Cinematic when describing the setting, playful during comedy, calm and reassuring during narration. Never overly theatrical.
