## Acceptance Criteria

- Does Ajolote sound like a convincing Mexican kid?
- Does Xolo sound sufficiently different?
- Does the Chilango dialogue feel natural?
- Are the emotional performances good?
- Do the characters remain recognizable?
- Does the pacing work for children?
- Are there too many sound effects?
- Does Grandma sound like a Grandma?

- Can I recognize the character immediately without hearing the name?
- Does Ajolote actually sound like a kid rather than an adult doing a kid voice?
- Does Xolo have enough personality that his dry lines are funny?
- Does Abuela sound genuinely Mexican rather than "generic Spanish grandmother"?
- Do the voices sound like they belong in the same world?
